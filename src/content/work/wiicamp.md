## The problem

A company website is read by two very different visitors: a prospective client skimming
on a phone between meetings, and a candidate reading every word on a desktop. Both have
to get a good experience out of one layout.

## What I did

I built the interface from Figma and the pieces that give the page its character: image
sliders, animated counters for company statistics, and a dark and light theme toggle. I
also wired up the API behind the email notification form so enquiries reach the team.

## Result

A site that holds its layout from a small phone up to a wide monitor, with a theme
preference that survives a page reload.
