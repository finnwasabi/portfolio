## The problem

Steel prices move daily and buyers make purchasing decisions on the trend, not the number.
The data was there; nobody could see the shape of it.

## What I did

I built the line charts showing yearly price statistics, so a buyer can see at a glance
whether today is a good day to buy, and the quotation feature that turns that judgement
into an actual purchase request.

## Result

Price history that reads in seconds instead of a spreadsheet that reads in minutes.
